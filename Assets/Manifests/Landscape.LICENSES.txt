PlagC valley character and surface sources

Human: Universal Base Characters, Quaternius (CC0 1.0).
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
Animations: Universal Animation Library, Quaternius (CC0 1.0).
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
Terrain surface: Grass 007, ambientCG (CC0 1.0).
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass007
Mirror revision and per-file SHA-256: Landscape.lock.json.
Modifications: bind-pose correction, directional aim clips derived offline, original uniform/armor/helmet, existing pinned Kenney weapon attachment.
Terrain, houses, needle-card texture, vegetation meshes and rocks are original procedural PlagC content.
